## Scope executed

Growing independently oversized raw, zlib-compressed and metadata-bearing
workloads (18M/36M rows per family) under one 256 MiB library root and an
enforced, self-checked soft/hard 512 MiB Linux RLIMIT_AS per non-forking worker.
Per case: pinned-native generation and full native validation, then three trials
of cold serial phase-timed scan, explicit build, strict reopen plus indexed full
ordered scan, 2/4/8-worker ordered parallel full scans, a 4-worker cancellation, a
2-worker head and a 2-worker zero scan, and a 4-worker cold-plus-two-warm parallel
run reusing one validated in-process session. Native, serial, indexed and every
parallel ordered digest, original IDs and special-value bits match; the cancelled
prefix is exactly one bounded batch (1024 rows) with the matching Python prefix
digest; zero limits launch no workers and read no record bytes. Actual stack
reservations (admitted × (2 MiB stack + 64 KiB guard)), control allowances, queued
output/reorder peaks (≤ admitted workers), joined workers, sampled descriptors
(≤ 4 including stdio), wait4 RSS and VmPeak are recorded per run in
[040-results.json](040-results.json).

## Findings and disposition

- On this 5-core host, admitted parallel full scans are workload-specific
  regressions versus serial for 4/8 workers (down to 0.47× on zlib, 0.10× on
  metadata) and at best neutral for 2 workers on 18M raw (1.04×). No parallel
  speedup is claimed; the serial result is not presented as parallel benefit.
  Task 039's measured policy consequently admits serial for full scans here and
  indexed for pruned queries (warm indexed full raw 36M: 11.32 s vs serial
  10.55 s; leading queries remain indexed-accelerated per task 036).
- Versus task 036's published 4M-row medians (raw 0.942 s full validating scan),
  this host's 4M-equivalent serial rate is slower per row on a 5-core sandbox;
  cross-machine absolute comparison is indicative only, like-for-like ratios
  above are the acceptance evidence. Break-even of build+reopen remains
  query-count dependent as in 035/036; no new break-even is claimed for parallel.
- Cancellation is prompt at the 1024-row polling granularity, joins all workers,
  and the acknowledged prefix digest matches; cleanup rechecks the source hash
  and retires owned sources/sidecars only after validation.
- Admitted concurrency equals requested 2/4/8 whenever enough intervals exist;
  stacks/queue slots are charged to the shared root before spawning.

## Limitations

Linux RLIMIT_AS/RSS/timings only; not cross-platform. One host, 5 cores;
results are not generalized to other workloads or machines. Cold uses advisory
per-file fsync/FADV_DONTNEED; device caches are uncontrolled. No automatic
execution policy change, parallel block compression, alternate codec, SIMD/unsafe
change, writer/metadata optimization, Polars integration, independent review or
automatic release is included or claimed.
